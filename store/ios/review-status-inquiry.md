## Where to send it

Contact Us → **App Review** → **App Review Status** (not Expedite):
<https://developer.apple.com/contact/app-store/>

This does not reorder the queue. It gets a human to look at the record, which is
what unsticks a submission that is genuinely stalled rather than merely queued.

## Do not do either of these

- **Do not cancel and resubmit.** Withdrawing the submission, or any metadata
  edit that forces a new one, sends you to the back of the queue. This is the
  most common way developers add days to their own wait.
- **Do not file an Expedited Review Request.** It qualifies only for a critical
  bug affecting released users or a time-sensitive public event. Neither applies
  to a first submission waiting in line, and spent requests count against future
  ones — keep it for the post-launch crash fix.
